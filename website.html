About SJCE

Sri Jayachamarajendra College of Engineering (SJCE), Mysore, was established in 1963 under JSS Mahavidyapeetha with an expansive 117-acre campus. It's recognized under TEQIP (Technical Education Quality Improvement Programme) and has held autonomous status since 2007–08.

Vision: “To be an international leader in engineering education, research and applying knowledge to benefit society globally.” Mission: Develop high-quality professionals, foster strong partnerships, and create empowered, identity-driven individuals. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Placements

SJCE boasts an impressive 90% placement rate with over 700 offers in recent years. The highest package recorded for B.Tech CSE stands at ₹56 LPA, with an average package of ₹7.4 LPA.
